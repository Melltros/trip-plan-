Day-by-Day Road Itinerary

Driving routes, recommended highway breaks, and temple coordinates.

Day 1: Kantaphod (MP) ➔ Mandsaur ➔ Sanwaliya Seth (Mandphiya) ~320 km • 6.5 – 7 hrs

Route: Kannod ➔ Dewas Bypass ➔ Ujjain Outer ➔ Jaora ➔ Mandsaur ➔ Neemuch ➔ Mandphiya

Key Stops
  • Mandsaur: Ashtamukhi Pashupatinath Temple on the Shivna river.
  • Mandphiya: Evening Sandhya Aarti at Shree Sanwaliya Seth Mandir.
Driving Tips

Depart Kantaphod by 6:00 AM to beat Dewas city traffic. Stop for lunch near Jaora on the four-lane highway.

Day 2: Mandphiya ➔ Chittorgarh Fort ~38 km • 45 min

Route: Mandphiya ➔ Bhadsora ➔ NH 27 ➔ Chittorgarh

Key Sights
  • Chittorgarh Fort: Vijay Stambha, Kirti Stambha, Padmini Palace, Gaumukh Reservoir.
  • Evening: Fort Light & Sound Show at 7:00 PM (Hindi).
Local Guidance

You can drive your vehicle inside the fort directly to each monument. An authorized guide costs approx. ₹400 for the group.

Day 3: Chittorgarh ➔ Nathdwara ➔ Udaipur ~145 km • ~3 hrs total

Route: Chittorgarh ➔ Mavli ➔ Nathdwara (NH 162) ➔ NH 58 ➔ Udaipur

Key Stops
  • Nathdwara: Shrinathji Temple Darshan & Vishwas Swaroopam (Statue of Belief).
  • Udaipur (Evening): Lake Pichola sunset, City Palace, Bagore Ki Haveli folk dance show.
Temple Guidelines

Mobile phones, cameras, and leather items are barred inside Shrinathji shrine. Leave them in your car.

Day 4 & 5: Udaipur ➔ Ujjain ➔ Return to Kantaphod ~330 km • ~6.5 hrs

Route: Udaipur ➔ Nimbahera ➔ Mandsaur ➔ Jaora ➔ Badnawar ➔ Ujjain

Spiritual Program
  • Day 4 Evening: Mahakal Darshan and evening walk along Mahakal Lok corridor.

Darshan Access

To avoid long general lines, buy the ₹250 Sheegra Darshan pass directly at Gate 4 counters.

Darshan & Travel Guidelines

Practical notes on dress codes, vehicle documentation, and timing.

Mahakaleshwar Ujjain

  • Bhasma Aarti (4 AM to 6 AM): Online booking is required at shrimahakaleshwar.com.
  • Sanctum Entry Dress Code: Unstitched cotton Dhoti with Solah for men; traditional Saree for women.
  • Day Visits: Regular VIP Darshan tickets (₹250) are sold in-person at Gate 4.

Shrinathji (Nathdwara)

  • Darshan is open only during designated Jhankis (Mangla, Rajbhog, Sandhya Aarti).
  • Strictly no electronic gadgets, mobile phones, leather wallets, or belts inside.
  • Use the designated Temple Trust locker counter.

Chittorgarh Fort

  • The perimeter is ~13 km. Always explore in your car rather than walking between gates.
  • Keep a valid ID card for all adults for ASI ticket counters.
  • Comfortable walking shoes are recommended for the towers.

Highway & Car Checklist

  • Maintain minimum ₹1,500 balance on your FASTag.
  • Carry physical and digital copies of RC, Insurance, and PUC.
  • Inspect tyre pressure, stepney spare tyre, and jack before departure.
