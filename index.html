Day-by-Day Route Guide

Highways, landmarks, driving times, and direct Google Maps links for each leg.

Day 1 — Kantaphod → Mandsaur → Sanwaliya Seth (Mandphiya)
~320 km · ~6.5–7 hrs driving
Route: Kantaphod → Kannod → Dewas Bypass → Ujjain Outer Ring → Jaora → Mandsaur → Neemuch → Mandphiya, Rajasthan
Key Stops
  • Mandsaur: Ashtamukhi Pashupatinath Temple on the banks of Shivna river — one of the few 8-faced Shiva Lingas in India.
  • Mandphiya (Sanwaliya Seth): Evening Sandhya Aarti darshan at the famous Shree Sanwaliya Seth Temple.
Driving Tip
  • Start at 6:00 AM to beat Dewas & Ujjain traffic.
  • Have lunch at the Bhopal–Jaora 4-lane dhaba stops.
  • Reach Mandphiya by 2–3 PM for parking before evening crowds.

Day 2 — Mandphiya → Chittorgarh Fort → Nathdwara
~145 km total · ~3–3.5 hrs driving
Route: Mandphiya → Bhadsora → NH27 Chittorgarh → Mavli → NH758 → Nathdwara
Chittorgarh Fort (~38 km, 45 min from Mandphiya)
  • Vijay Stambha, Kirti Stambha, Padmini Palace, Gaumukh Reservoir.
  • Drive your car inside the fort (13 km perimeter) — no need to walk between monuments.
  • Allow 3–4 hours for a comfortable visit.
Nathdwara (~100 km, 2 hrs from Chittorgarh)
  • Shrinathji Temple Darshan — aim for Rajbhog (11 AM) or Sandhya Aarti (5 PM) slot.
  • Vishwas Swaroopam (Statue of Belief) — 369 ft Shiva statue.
  • No phones or leather items inside temple. Use car locker.

Temple & Driving Guidelines

Critical tips to avoid delays and issues at temples, forts, and state borders.

Mahakaleshwar — Ujjain

  • Bhasma Aarti (4 AM): Book online at shrimahakaleshwar.com. Spots fill 2–3 weeks in advance.
  • Dress code inside sanctum: Unstitched white dhoti (Solah) for men; Saree for women.
  • Day Sheegra Darshan: ₹250 counter pass at Gate 4. Saves 2–3 hours over general queue.
  • Mahakal Lok: Best after 7 PM when lights are on. Free entry.

Shrinathji — Nathdwara

  • Darshan only during 8 Jhankis: Mangla (~5 AM), Rajbhog (~11 AM), Sandhya (~5 PM).
  • Strictly prohibited: Mobile phones, cameras, leather wallets, belts, shoes inside shrine.
  • Use the temple trust cloakroom/locker counter outside Gate 1.

Sanwaliya Seth — Mandphiya

  • Temple open 5 AM – 11 PM. Weekends and Ekadashi days are very crowded.
  • Large designated parking lot 200 m from temple gate (free).
  • All food stalls near the temple are pure satvik / vegetarian.

Chittorgarh Fort

  • Drive into the fort — parking is available near each major monument.
  • Minimum 3.5 hrs to cover the main sights. Carry water.
  • Light & Sound Show (Hindi): 7:00 PM, near Rana Kumbha Palace (~₹100/head).
  • Hire a guide at the entry gate: ~₹400–500 for the group.

MP–Rajasthan Border Checks

  • Neemuch / Nimbahera check posts often verify RC, Insurance, PUC for non-RJ vehicles.
  • Keep physical + digital copies of all vehicle documents.
  • FASTag balance: top up to minimum ₹2,000 before departure.

Indore – Day 5 Food Stops

  • Sarafa Bazaar: Best street food market in MP (evening onwards).
  • Rajwada Chowk: Poha-Jalebi for breakfast — the authentic Indore morning ritual.
  • Indore → Kantaphod via AB road: ~180 km, ~3.5 hrs.
