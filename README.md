# 6-Person Road Trip: MP to Rajasthan & Ujjain Circuit

A practical, minimalist road trip guide, itinerary, interactive budget calculator, and verified affordable hotel directory for 6 adults traveling from **Kantaphod (MP) ➔ Mandsaur ➔ Sanwaliya Seth (Mandphiya) ➔ Chittorgarh ➔ Nathdwara ➔ Udaipur ➔ Ujjain ➔ Kantaphod**.

## 🗺️ Route Overview
- **Total Circuit Distance:** ~890 km
- **Duration:** 4 Nights / 5 Days
- **Recommended Vehicle:** 6-7 Seater (Innova / Ertiga / Scorpio / Safari)
- **Master Route Map:** [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Kantaphod,+Madhya+Pradesh/Pashupatinath+Temple,+Mandsaur/Shree+Sanwaliya+Seth+Temple,+Mandphiya/Chittorgarh+Fort,+Chittorgarh/Shrinathji+Temple,+Nathdwara/Lake+Pichola,+Udaipur/Mahakaleshwar+Jyotirlinga,+Ujjain/Kantaphod,+Madhya+Pradesh)

## 📅 Day-by-Day Schedule
- **Day 1:** Kantaphod ➔ Mandsaur (Pashupatinath Temple) ➔ Mandphiya (Sanwaliya Seth Mandir) (~320 km)
- **Day 2:** Mandphiya ➔ Chittorgarh Fort (~38 km)
- **Day 3:** Chittorgarh ➔ Nathdwara (Shrinathji Temple) ➔ Udaipur (Lake Pichola & City Palace) (~145 km)
- **Day 4:** Udaipur ➔ Ujjain (Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga & Mahakal Lok Corridor) (~330 km)
- **Day 5:** Ujjain (4:00 AM Bhasma Aarti / Kal Bhairav) ➔ Return to Kantaphod (~130 km)

## 🏨 Affordable Accommodations for 6 (₹1,600 – ₹3,500 total / night)
- **Mandphiya (Night 1):** Shri Sanwaliya Dham Atithi Grah (Trust) / Hotel Lakhdatar
- **Chittorgarh (Night 2):** Hotel Soni Inn / Hotel Aadi Mata & Banquets
- **Udaipur (Night 3):** Mewar Haveli (Standard Wing) / Hotel Paras Mahal
- **Ujjain (Night 4):** Pt. Suryanarayan Vyas Atithi Niwas (Mahakal Trust) / Hotel Satyam (6-Bed Family Suite)
